Temporary residence permit

Everything you need to get your application started

How it works

Six steps from application to collecting your permit.

  1. 1

    Start your application

    Submit your request online

    Complete the online application form on the official migration portal. Step-by-step instructions are available here.

    Check which permit type fits your situation and make sure you meet the eligibility requirements.

    We can help with the practical steps of the process, but we can't advise you on which permit category to choose.

  2. 2

    Pay the fees

    See what you need to pay and how

    The government fee is paid directly to the relevant tax authority, either online or at a bank.

    A separate service fee is paid in local currency when you attend your appointment.

    Fees are not refunded, whatever the outcome of your application.

  3. 3

    Book an appointment

    Pick a centre and a time that suits you

    Once your online application is submitted, book an appointment to give your biometrics. A confirmation email will follow — bring it with you on the day.

    Applying as a family or group? Each person needs their own appointment, and you should mention that you're part of a group.

  4. 4

    Attend your appointment

    Give your biometrics in person

    Your identity will be checked against the documents uploaded with your online application, so bring the originals. If you paid the government fee online, make sure the payment receipt is attached to your application.

    After your biometrics are taken, the migration authority may invite you to a short remote interview.

    Read what to expect on the day.

  5. 5

    Follow your application

    Check progress at any time

    Progress updates and the final decision appear in your online portal account and are also sent by email.

    Centre staff can't share any extra information about where your application is in the process.

  6. 6

    Collect your permit

    Bring the ID document you applied with

    When your permit card arrives, collect it in person and bring your original passport so your identity can be confirmed.

    Check the centre's opening hours before you go.
